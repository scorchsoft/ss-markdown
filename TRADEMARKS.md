## If you publish a fork

1. Give it a different name, one that uses neither "Scorchsoft" nor
   "SS Markdown".
2. Give it a different icon: replace the launcher icon resources and
   remove `docs/brand/` and `docs/store/`.
3. Remove Scorchsoft from the app's own branding, including "Made by
   Scorchsoft" on the About screen. Keep the copyright line and NOTICE
   attributions.
4. Change the `applicationId`.
